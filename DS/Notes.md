### Boolean Algebra
- A.B.C + A.B.C = A.B.C
- $\overline{A.B.C}$ + A.B.C = 1
- $\overline{A.B}$ + A.B = 1
- A + BC = (A+B) (A+C)
- A+ $\overline{A}$B = A + B
- AB+CD = (AB + C) (AB+D)

> [!Important] De Morgan's Theorem

- $\overline{XYZ}$ = $\overline{X}$ + $\overline{Y}$ + $\overline{Z}$ 
- $\overline{X+Y+Z}$ = $\overline{X}$ . $\overline{Y}$ . $\overline{Z}$
