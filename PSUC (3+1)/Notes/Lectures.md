> [!info] First unit for 16 marks

Octal number: Digits lie from 0-7 **only**
Decimal number: 0-9
Hexadecimal: 16 digits, 0-9, 10 (A), 11 (B), 12 (C), 13 (D), 14 (E), 15 (F)

**Representation:**
Binary number: (1111101)<sub>2</sub>
Octal number: (175)<sub>8</sub>
Hexadecimal number: (FA8)<sub>16</sub> 

**For Octal to Binary:**
- Use the 4-2-1 method, where you check, for example, if the octal digit is "4", then you will only add "4", skipping 2 and 1, so your binary will be 100 since binary here will be of 3 bits. If octal digit is "3", then we will skip 4, add 2 and 1, making our binary 011.
  
**For Hexadecimal to Binary:**
- Use the 8-4-2-1 method, similar to "Octal to Binary."
